Spot finder

  • Opens on Favourites, now. Press "Where" to switch to every spot near you.
  • One hour. Press or drag along the timeline (a finger taps it, and swipes it sideways for more days). Every card, pin and the wind on the map change to that hour.
  • Cards: satellite picture with the ride rose, stars, the wind, and the day's wind as a bar with the hour marked. Hover a card and its pin grows; press a pin for its card.
  • The map draws the wind in ink, as the chart does, from the data the chart already has.
