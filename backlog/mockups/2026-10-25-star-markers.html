4 · Before we build it

Questions for you

  1. The gradient. For five to stand apart in the full magenta, one to four need to be lighter than they are on the spots page today. The spots page would change to match. Compare them with 1–4 in the bar.
  2. Five's shape. Star in a star (the shape you liked on four), Solid (the same, filled), or the first round's Black disc.
  3. Four's shape. A plain star, so only five has the special shape, or a star in a star too, so four and five both stand out.
  4. Can you tell one, two and three apart? The star grows a little with each one as well as deepening. Is that enough, or should they carry the number?
  5. No hollow star for one. An outline already means a star the hour lost to something (gusts, a cross-offshore wind…). The rating plan defines it.
  6. How much motion? The proposal: four breathes slowly, five pumps like a heartbeat. With lots of good spots on a windy day, the chart could get busy. 5 only keeps the movement for the best. Motion stops for anyone with reduced motion turned on, and while the map is being dragged.
  7. The open spot's wedge. Today the selected spot shows a pulsing wedge of the headings you can hold. The plan is to keep it, pulsing behind the new light.
  8. The app. The phone app's map would get the same lights in the same release.
