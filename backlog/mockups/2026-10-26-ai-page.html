wingfoil.ie in your AI app

Ask your AI about the wind.

wingfoil.ie is built to work inside the AI apps you already use. Add it once, and your AI can read our live forecast for spots all around Ireland, so you can ask about the wind, the tide and where to go in your own words.

  • Free
  • No account needed
  • About a minute to set up

Which AI app do you use?

Choose your app and we'll show you how to add wingfoil.ie to it. You only need to do this once.

          What to look at

          • The header is the Spot finder's, measured off /spots: the 40px round menu button, the small wordmark (19px) beside it, the double rule under it and the same padding (12/18, 10/12 on a phone). "| AI" and "← Chart" are gone. One difference: /spots hides the wordmark on a phone to make room for Favourites / Near you; this page has nothing on the right, so the wordmark stays. Say if you'd rather it went.
          • No coding agents, and no numbered steps. The page reads top to bottom: what it is, which app you use, what to press, then what to ask.
          • One button per app, the same for every app: "Add to ChatGPT", "Add to Claude" and so on. In B it is the page's one filled button and only the chosen app's is shown; in A every app has the same ruled button. Under it, in plain words, what happens when you press it, and a shaded box with the exact words you'll see in that app, in bold.
          • No more "Ask" button per app. The questions are shown apart, as things to try once wingfoil.ie is added. In B they open in the chosen app (and say so, and that it only works once it's added); in A they are copied, to paste into whichever app you added.
          • Gemini, Meta AI and Copilot can't add wingfoil.ie at all. In B they sit behind "Another app", which says so plainly; in A they are a single line under the list.
          • The example chat beside the headline shows what you get, so nobody has to understand "connectors" to see the point. Its forecast is made up.
          • Words. "Address" for the link, "add" for connecting. "MCP" only appears where the app itself uses it on its own button (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Le Chat), in bold as a word to look for.
          • Why B. A rider uses one app. Choosing it first means the page then shows one button and one short set of instructions, so there's nothing to compare and nothing to wonder about. A is quicker to scan but is back to eight-ish buttons on one screen.
