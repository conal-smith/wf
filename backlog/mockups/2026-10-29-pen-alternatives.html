After the red pen

Three cleaner ways to mark the chosen hour, the time now and the chosen spot. Tap an hour or a spot to move the mark.

Today

The red pen

Hand-drawn loops, twice round the hour; a wobbly red line at now.

Option A

Ink tab

A quiet sunken column ruled in ink, the hour in an ink pill. It slides between hours. Closest to the chart look.

Option B

Magenta focus

A crisp rounded ring in the chart's magenta with a soft glow. Now is a red dot and line, as in a calendar app.

Option C

Lifted card

The chosen hour rises on a paper card that springs from hour to hour. The pin lifts off the chart.
