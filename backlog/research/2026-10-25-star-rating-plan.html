One star rating for wind and gusts

A plan for you to decide. Nothing in the app has changed. "Today" below is the default rider on the site now (Riding, 80 kg).

The recommendation

  • One rating for everyone, "ours". No more Learning, Riding and Advanced. Riders who want a different rating make their own from ours.
  • Five stars at 15–22 kn, then a steep drop. Four at 23–25, three at 27, two at 28, one at 30, none from 32. Most riders struggle above 25–28 kn.
  • Gusts are read 5 kn lighter on the same scale, and the hour gets the lower score. 15 g25 and 20 g25 get five stars. 20 g30 gets four. 22 g35 gets one.
  • Outline stars only for gusts and the shore angle. Your wings never cost a star; if they don't suit the hour, you get a note instead.
  • Drop the "how much does gusty wind bother you" setting. The editor below replaces it.

The two numbers. The wind (steady wind) is the forecast's average wind, the 10-minute mean at 10 m. The gust is the strongest few-second burst in the hour.

What an outline star means

The solid stars are what the hour is worth. Each outline is one star lost to one named thing: the gusts, or the shore angle (cross-offshore or offshore). Tap or hover to see which. Solid plus outlines is what the steady wind alone would give.

Your wings never cost a star. If they don't suit the hour, a note says so. An orange or red warning replaces the stars. Gusts alone can still take an hour to zero, and then it shows no outlines.

What the research says

  • Sweet spot

    About 14–20 kn for most riders, and up to 25 kn as they improve.WindUp, MACkite, Nootica (14–20 / 16–20 / 15–20); FoilPlanet (15–25).

  • 25 kn

    Where it starts to get hard. "Most recreational riders start struggling above 25–28 kt." A 5 m wing tops out at 25 kn.WindUp; F-One Strike V6 chart.

  • 30 kn

    A limit, not a good day. One sailing club stops all sailing when it is "consistently gusting over 30 knots". One school's wing-foiling window ends at 30. A forum rider says 30–40 kn is "possible to control but not much fun".Chew Valley Lake SC; Northern Kites; Seabreeze (excerpt).

  • Gusts

    Riders judge the gust in knots: how strong it gets. "15 to 20 with the sporadic gust to 25" is "more manageable than … gusts up to 30". Pick your wing size for the gusts.MACkite; WindUp.

What our own data shows

  • At open sea, the weather buoys measure gusts about 30% above the steady wind, and the forecasts agree: 20 kn steady, gusts about 26.
  • At our coastal spots, the forecasts put the gusts 60–90% above the steady wind (15 kn steady, gusts 24–28), because a coastal forecast point is partly over land. So 15 g25 is a normal day there. In September and October, today's rule took a star off two riding hours in three for being "gusty". It took nearly all the rest (29%) to zero for gusts "over your limit".
  • We checked the forecasts against what was actually measured: at four weather buoys, and hour by hour at three Met Éireann weather stations (Malin Head, Belmullet, Dublin Airport). The forecast gets the gust's size about right, as well as it gets the steady wind. But it can't tell which hours will be gustier than usual. So a rule based on the ratio is mostly guessing, and a rule based on the gust in knots is reliable.

From the gust research (PR #202). The buoys measured September and October 2026. The stations were checked hour by hour from September 2025 to March 2026.

For you to decide

  1. Rider weight. Today a heavier rider's scale moves up a little. Keep that, or leave it to each rider to adjust their own?
  2. Riders on Learning or Advanced, or with their own rating today. Suggested: Learning and Advanced riders move to ours, and an existing own rating is converted into the four settings.
  3. Shore angle. Suggested: cross-offshore costs 1 star and offshore costs 4, as Riding has today.
  4. Should the editor go further? Limits on the settings: five stars must last at least 1 kn, and the stars must run out at least 4 kn after five ends. Should we also offer a fifth setting for where four stars end? Today that's fixed at 3 kn after five ends.
