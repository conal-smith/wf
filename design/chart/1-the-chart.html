      About this chart

      Every spot is a light: the more stars its best hour has, the more it flashes. Drag the tide-table along the bottom to move the wind and the lights through the week. Pick a spot to see its hours; the red pen circles the window worth driving for.

      Hour-by-hour ratings, tides and the fine bay surveys cover the five surveyed spots in this mockup. The other 141 show each day's best hour, and the wind the lines are drawn from.
