Four directions for wingfoil.ie

Each mockup answers the same three questions, on real forecast data for 5–9 October 2026 with the v2 stars (80 kg, riding): how the map shows where it's on, what a spot page looks like (stars per day and per hour, the wing, the tide and daylight), and how a companion helps.

Every file opens directly in a browser. Add #map, #spot/brandon or #companion to jump to a view. The background is in README.md.

Shipping Forecast

Open

An Admiralty chart crossed with a broadsheet. Paper, ink, chart magenta; calm authority.

Map Spot page · Brandon Bay The Almanac
MapDepth contours offset from the coast, wind barbs per spot. Four-star spots flash magenta like a lighthouse: Fl(4)Mg 8s. The time scrubber is a tide table.
Spot pageA generated headline ("a four-star Tuesday evening on a 6.5 m"), the best window circled in red pen, and footnotes for every star taken off.
CompanionAn ink puffin that answers by annotating the forecast itself: it circles hours, pins spots and brackets rows.

Isobar

Open

3am over the Atlantic, with the wind made visible. Cinematic, it continues the three.js intro film.

Map Spot page · Brandon Bay Gust
MapAbout 9,000 particles flow on the wind field for the selected hour; spots are beacons that glow with their stars. Clicking one dives into the spot.
Spot pageThe Ribbon: five days in 3D (height is wind, twist is direction, colour is stars, a pink skin for gusts), scrolled through like a time bar.
CompanionA small cyclone of particles; its answers send particles flying to the spots it names.

Telemetry

A chartplotter crossed with an F1 pit wall. Numbers first; lime is kept for "go".

Map Spot page · Brandon Bay Co-pilot
MapA dot-matrix Ireland, a bar gauge of rideable wind per spot, a crosshair with live readouts, J/K between spots and a "rig now" leaderboard.
Spot pageA seven-segment countdown to the next ★4 window, a speed trace with gust shading, and a rider config that re-scores every hour live.
CompanionNo face: a terminal line that answers in radio calls ("BRANDON ★4 FROM 18:00. 6.5 M. GO.") and moves the cursor.

Map Spot page · Brandon Bay Puffin
MapA layered papercut coast, wind squiggles drifting downwind, and sticker riders whose pose is the rating, from a flask on the beach to flying with spray.
Spot pageThe beach strip: an hourly comic with the wing drawn to the hour's size, stars stamped on, and "SEND IT" on the good ones.
CompanionA puffin on a foil whose kit follows the weather; it answers with cards and "Take me there" buttons.
