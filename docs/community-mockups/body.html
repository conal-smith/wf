wingfoil.ie · design review · 20 September 2026

Community: channels and threads, in the Atlantic palette

Five desktop views and three phone views, scoped to the first iteration. The site sets the channels; members start threads inside them; a thread is one flat, live conversation with reply-to references and reactions rather than a tree of nested comments. Every colour, space, radius and type step is a Force5 --sem-* token, and the page is built from the real bundle CSS.

1 · Desktop · /community

Home: one feed, so a small community reads as one busy room

Latest activity across every channel, with the channel named on each row. Two columns: channels and the conversation, no side panel. The member count appears from 50 members. Every channel is set up by the site.

2 · Desktop · /community/wings

A channel: threads sorted by last activity, filtered by tag

One pane, rows divided by hairlines, in one order: most recent activity first, so a reply bumps a thread. Tags are the only filter. Unread is carried by weight, a dot and a count, never by colour alone. The head shows two counts: threads and followers.

3 · Desktop · /community/wings/5m-or-6m-one-wing-quiver

A thread: a flat, live conversation

The opening post is the one card; replies stream below it on the ground, in time order. A reply can point at an earlier message, carry photos and reactions, and the thread can link a spot to show its live wind. Here the composer’s Reply is the filled action, so the header’s New thread goes quiet.

I’ve been out about 25 times on a rented 5m and I’m buying my first wing. I’m 82 kg on a 95 L board with a 1600 cm² front foil, riding mostly Dollymount and Malahide. Most of my days are 14 to 22 knots.

Do I get a 6m and accept being overpowered on the windy days, or a 5m and sit out the light ones? The budget is one wing until spring.

Dollymount, Co. Dublin WSW · gusting 26 kn · low water 14:10 19 kn now

Cian MThursday at 20:05

At 82 kg on that foil, 5m. You’ll be flying from 15 knots once your pumping improves, and a 6m over 25 at Dollymount is a handful in the shore break. Fix the light days with a bigger foil, not a bigger wing.

Following and notifications
